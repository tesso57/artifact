# Interview question bank

Pick the few that matter; offer options instead of open questions where you can.

## Job
- Who opens this, and when? (a) daily check-in (b) weekly review (c) on demand when X happens
- What should they be able to decide or do after looking at it?
- What do they do today instead (spreadsheet, five browser tabs, nothing)?

## Data
- Which system holds the data? (I found these MCP tools: …) Which one is it?
- Which 3 numbers would you put at the top?
- What makes an item need attention (overdue, failing, over budget)?
- What changes between uses: (a) project/repo (b) date range (c) person/team (d) thresholds?

## Interaction
- Filter or group by what?
- Clicking a row should (a) show details (b) open the source link (c) run an action
- Anything to write back (MCP action) or to remember (notes, checkmarks, settings)?

## Look
- Dense table or roomy cards?
- Must it work on a phone?
- Will it be exported and shared as an HTML file?

## Good `useCases` (they route future requests)

- "weekly review of open pull requests and their reviewers"
- "track daily habits or a personal checklist that persists"
- "compare a metric across teams or categories with a bar chart"

Avoid vague ones like "dashboard" or "useful page".
